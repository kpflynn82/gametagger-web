Not a genre on its own, but the strongest multiplier for success. Players must convince 3–5 friends to buy → built-in viral loop. Cheap price ($10–15) makes the ask easy. Slapstick physics + streaming-friendly = organic marketing. R.E.P.O. ($147M) and PEAK ($87M) prove this.

The Key Insight

The single biggest factor for a low-budget hit isn't just genre — it's genre + multiplier. The winning formula in 2024–2025 is: pick a high-hit-rate genre (horror, sim, survival craft) and add co-op multiplayer. Co-op creates a viral purchase loop (every player needs their own copy) and generates streaming/social media content organically. Horror + co-op alone accounts for a disproportionate share of indie megahits.

Low-poly and retro visuals (PS1 aesthetic, pixel art in non-platformer contexts) are actually advantages — they reduce production costs while being seen as stylistic choices rather than budget limitations.

Also notable: experience compounds. A developer's second game sells 40% better on average, and their third sells 24% better than that. The studios behind Palworld, Lethal Company, and V Rising had all shipped games before.
